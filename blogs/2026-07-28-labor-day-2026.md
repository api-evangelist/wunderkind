Labor Day has long marked summer's unofficial finale. For consumers, it's one last barbecue, one final getaway, and a chance to tackle home projects before autumn arrives. For retailers, however, it represents one of the biggest shopping weekends before the holiday season, and an opportunity to capture valuable customer relationships that extend far beyond a single long weekend.
